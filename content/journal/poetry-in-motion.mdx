The morning light has a specific kind of quiet to it.

It doesn't rush. It doesn't demand attention. It simply arrives, stretching across the floorboards with a patience we often forget to practice.

> We spend so much time looking for big moments that we forget how much beauty exists in the small ones.

I've started keeping a small notebook just to write down these passing thoughts. Not full stories, just small observations. 

A bird resting on the balcony.

The smell of coffee before the house wakes up.

The way the shadows change when the season shifts.

These aren't profound, earth-shattering realizations. But they ground me. They remind me that even on the busiest days, there is poetry happening all around us if we just stop long enough to notice it.
