### Overview
Developed a customized attendance management solution in Odoo to streamline employee check-in/check-out tracking, automate attendance calculations, and simplify HR operations across multiple departments.

### Problem
The organization relied on manual attendance processes, resulting in inaccurate working hours, payroll discrepancies, and increased administrative effort.

### Solution
Built a centralized attendance system that automated employee attendance tracking, validated work hours, managed overtime calculations, and generated attendance reports for HR teams.

### Key Features
- Employee Check In / Check Out
- Attendance Validation
- Overtime Calculation
- Leave Integration
- Monthly Attendance Reports
- Multi-company Support
- Employee Dashboard
- HR Analytics

### Challenges
- Handling overnight shifts correctly.
- Managing timezone differences.
- Synchronizing attendance with leave records.
- Optimizing report performance for large datasets.

### Results
- Reduced manual attendance processing by over 80%
- Improved payroll accuracy
- Faster HR reporting
- Better employee attendance visibility

### What I Learned
- HR module customization
- Attendance business workflows
- Performance optimization
- Complex business rule implementation
